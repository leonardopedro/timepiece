## 2. What was missing, and is now proved

Five new modules, all `sorry`-free; the axiom audit is
`Work/GaugeSymmetryAudit.lean` (only `propext`, `Classical.choice`,
`Quot.sound`).

### `BookProof/ChapterGaugeVariantVanishing.lean`
Book 6730–6780: *"there is no spontaneous symmetry breaking of the (full or
global) gauge symmetry, since the expectation-values of the gauge-variant
operators [vanish]"*.

* `expectation_eq_smul_of_covariant`, `expectation_gauge_variant_eq_zero`: in a
  state invariant under a gauge unitary, every operator rescaled by a factor
  `≠ 1` by that unitary has expectation value `0`.
* `no_spontaneous_gauge_symmetry_breaking`: the charged-operator form, for a
  phase `e^{iθ}` with `θ` not a multiple of `2π`.
* `expectation_gauge_invariant_operator`: gauge-invariant observables (e.g. the
  global charge) have the same expectation value on every gauge copy of a state.

### `BookProof/ChapterGaugeAdjointAlgebra.lean`
Book 7290–7400: the Weyl-gauge Hamiltonian `H_W = ½π² + ½B²`, its remnant gauge
symmetry generated by the Gauss law `θ_a (D_i π^i)_a`, and its positivity. The
gauge algebra is an arbitrary real Lie algebra; the coordinates representing
spatial derivatives are independent variables, as everywhere in this
development.

* `adj_closure`: the infinitesimal gauge transformations close (Jacobi).
* `covariantDeriv_covariant`: covariance of `D_i X = ∂_i X + ⁅A_i, X⁆` under a
  local gauge transformation, given the Leibniz gauge-fixing rule for the
  derivative coordinates.
* `gaussLaw_covariant`, `gaussLaw_constraint_surface_invariant`: the Gauss law
  transforms in the adjoint, so `G = 0` is gauge invariant.
* `magnetic_covariant`: `B_{ij} = ∂_i A_j − ∂_j A_i + ⁅A_i, A_j⁆` transforms in
  the adjoint (the inhomogeneous terms cancel by symmetry of the
  second-derivative coordinates).
* `weylEnergy_gauge_invariant`, `weylEnergy_nonneg`: the Weyl-gauge energy has
  vanishing gauge variation and is non-negative — the book's *"positive definite
  in the Weyl gauge"*.

### `BookProof/ChapterGaugeWeylResidual.lean`
Book 7364–7375 and 2294: the Weyl gauge is an *incomplete* gauge fixing whose
remnant consists of the gauge parameters constant in time.

* `weyl_residual_iff_time_independent`: preserving `A₀ = 0` ⇔ `∂_t θ = 0`.
* `remnant_moves_every_configuration`: the remnant acts freely on the spatial
  gauge field.
* `weyl_gauge_fixing_incomplete`: two distinct configurations of the Weyl
  surface are gauge equivalent.

### `BookProof/ChapterGaugeMechanicsCharge.lean`
Book 2402–2455 (and 7080–7120): the gauge-mechanics model on `L²(ℝ² × ℤ₂)`,
realized on the polynomial core `ℂ[φ, φ*]`.

* `ccr_phi_pi`, `ccr_phi_piStar`: `[φ, π] = i`, `[φ, π*] = 0`.
* `chargeQ_eq_euler`, `chargeQ_homogeneous`: the charge `Q = πφ + π*φ*` is
  `-i(E + 2)`, with charge `-i(n+2)` on configurations of degree `n`.
* `chargeQ_not_commute_field`: `[Q, φ] = -i φ` — the gauge generator is excluded
  from the commutative algebra of the fields (unconstrained gauge fixing).
* `mul_commutes_chargeQ_iff_euler_zero`, `bilinear_commutes_chargeQ`: the
  gauge-invariant algebra contains no non-constant function of the fields, but
  does contain the degree-preserving bilinears.
* `brst_nilpotent`, `brst_ne_zero`, `ghost_car`: `Ω = Q ψ†` is nilpotent and
  non-zero, with `{ψ, ψ†} = 1`.

### `BookProof/ChapterGhostMajoranaRep.lean`
Book 6940–6960: the two presentations of the ghost algebra.

* `psi_not_selfAdjoint` versus `chi1_selfAdjoint`, `chi2_selfAdjoint`;
* `chi1_sq`, `chi2_sq`, `chi_anticomm`: the Clifford relations;
* `psi_of_chi`, `psiDag_of_chi`: the inverse change of basis, so the fermionic
  and self-adjoint presentations generate the same algebra.

## 3. What is deliberately still prose

* The *interpretive* parts of the gauge chapter (Egison patterns, the discussion
  of the BFV path integral, Dirac's remark on the standard ket) are commentary,
  not mathematical statements.
* Reconstruction of gauge potentials from gauge-invariant functions (Giles) and
  the in–in/in–out discussion are cited results of the literature, not proved
  here.
* The Standard-Model Yukawa and CKM bookkeeping is formalized only through the
  already existing parity/custodial/hypercharge modules; the full Lagrangian is
  not formalized.
* No spectral information, mass gap or continuum limit is claimed anywhere by
  the modules above.
